(b) No likelihoods for truncated (measurement threshold) and grouped (class frequency) data; if a threshold is declared, no fit is made. 14. Relative Ranking gaps: (a) Criteria cannot be given different weights (equal weights as in the paper). (c) Criteria giving identical ranks are only reported; high but not perfect association (e.g. RMSE–MAE) is not reported. (d) The radar has at most 18 distinguishable line styles; more would be crowded.

15. Height–Diameter Modelling gaps (fixed-effects version): (a) No mixed-effects models (plot random effect, EBLUP calibration with a few measured heights in a new plot); for this reason SE, intervals and AIC are not given for multi-plot data. (d) Predicted heights are not written to the approved data package (export only). (e) The "50 × max(h − bh)" divergence threshold for the asymptote is a tool rule. (f) Michailoff = "Schumacher" mapping not verified [D]. (g) Species can be fitted one by one; no common model with a species effect.

16. Inventory Sampling Planner gaps (first version): (a) z approximation only; no iterative computation with t (the number comes out slightly low for small n; stated on screen). (d) Uncertainty of the pilot CV (e.g. confidence interval for CV) not reflected in the result. (e) No allowance for inaccessible plots and cost. (f) No choice of plot size/shape. (g) Cochran (1977) reference [D].

17. Citation: (a) ✔ DOI exists: Zenodo concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24135603 (all versions); the version DOIs of v2026.11.0 and v2026.11.1 in `CITATION.cff`.
