# Forest Biometrics Tools v2026.11.2

Fifteen new tools since v2026.11.1: the platform now has **24 tools in eight modules**, from data checking and tree
measurement to stem volume, biomass, stand structure, site productivity and statistical modelling. Every result can be
saved as a sealed **run record** and checked later. Coefficients stay yours: no species or regional coefficients are
built in; the tools that need them read your sets from a shared **Coefficient Library**, each with its source. A new
home page is built for finding a tool, and every browser test now runs before each upload.

The calculations of the tools that were in v2026.11.1 are unchanged; their new minor versions add the run record
(and, in the Forest Data Checker, approval into the Inventory Data Manager), so citations of the earlier versions stay
valid. New tools start at 1.0.0-beta.1; the beta number grows when the methods a tool computes change.

## Data infrastructure

- **Coefficient Library:** every tool's coefficient sets in one place, with their sources; filters, problems,
  duplicates, copies, a full backup and a restore that shows its plan before applying it.
- **Run records** (`run-record/1`) from every tool: tool, version, the site's source commit, settings, results and the
  SHA-256 fingerprint of each input file, sealed. The **Record Verifier** opens a record and checks its seal, versions
  and, given the files, the inputs.
- **Inventory Data Manager:** several approved data packages, one active; plot × period overview; packages move
  between browsers as JSON files.
- **Visualization Dashboard:** filtered panels from a package, with curves taken from run records.
- **Report Generator:** a printable report (PDF through the browser, or HTML) from a package and run records.

## Tree measurement

- **Tree Measurement:** height (tangent, rangefinder, shadow and two-pole methods, slope correction), diameter and
  basal area (girth, two diameters, ellipse, tree list), bark (gauge readings, two girths, bark factor and line from
  sample trees, your bark equations), crown (projection from radii or two diameters, crown length and ratio, crown
  surface and volume, crown cover), form factors, form exponent and form quotient, tree age, increment cores and growth
  percent. Formulas from Hamilton (1975), Graves (1906), van Laar and Akça (2007) and Kershaw et al. (2016).

## Stem volume and taper

- **Stem Taper:** Max and Burkhart (1976) and Kozak (2004) with your coefficients or fitted to your stem data;
  merchantable volume to a top diameter and a log-length limit; bucking into your products by priority or by value
  (dynamic programming).
- **Volume Equations:** constant form factor, combined variable, logarithmic and local equations fitted to your trees
  of known volume (e.g. Stem Section Volume results), with weights and a back-transformation correction; saved to the
  Coefficient Library and sent to Model Evaluation.
- **Log Scaling:** board feet by the Scribner, Doyle and International (⅛ and ¼ inch) rules and m³ by Smalian, Huber
  and Newton; logs longer than the scaling length are split.

## Biomass and carbon

- **Biomass and Carbon:** component biomass with your allometric equations or from volume through density and
  expansion factors; carbon and CO₂ (44/12) with your carbon fraction; additive component systems by restricted SUR
  and nonlinear SUR (Parresol 1999, 2001); Monte Carlo uncertainty; volumes from Stem Section Volume.

## Stand structure, site and modelling

- **Stand Structure & Competition:** diameter structure, Gini and Lorenz curve, Shannon and Simpson, SDI, Curtis RD,
  relative spacing and the crown competition factor (CCF, with your crown-width or crown-area equations); with
  coordinates, Clark–Evans, W, M, T, T1, U and the Hegyi index with the edge correction you choose.
- **Growth Function Fitter:** Chapman–Richards, Schumacher, Gompertz, logistic, monomolecular and Weibull fitted to
  your age–size data; AIC and RMSE; current and mean annual increment, inflection point and culmination.
- **Nonlinear Regression:** ready-made forms or your own formula; weights, intervals, AIC.
- **Mixed-Effects Models:** linear and nonlinear, ML or REML, and the calibration of a new plot from a few
  measurements; checked against `nlme`.
- **Systems of Equations:** your own formulas with shared coefficients by OLS, SUR or iterated SUR; checked against
  `systemfit`.

## Site

- **Home page** rebuilt for finding a tool: search (Turkish letters and case ignored, both languages and catalogue
  ids indexed), module filters, task filters taken from the user guide, recently used tools, and every tool visible as
  a card without a click.
- **User guide** split into one page per tool with a section tree, search and task cards; a troubleshooting section
  (pages that do not load because of QUIC/HTTP/3, and how to turn it off in the main browsers).
- **References** page: every source in APA 7, with the tools that use it and its reading status.
- The planned tools that were not built were removed from the plan; the site lists only tools that exist.
- **Tests:** every scientific calculation is tested against an independent reference (SciPy, NumPy, mpmath,
  scikit-learn, R packages run by the author, or hand-computed cases). New in this release: all browser tests run in
  CI before each upload, against the bundled files the server gets and against the sources.
